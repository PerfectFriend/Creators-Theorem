# Language Strategy

## Goal

Make the cycle readable by **at least 75% of the world’s adult population**.

## Priority languages (Phase 1)

| Priority | Language | Code | Approx. speakers (native + L2) | Notes |
|----------|----------|------|--------------------------------|-------|
| 1 | English | `en` | ~1.5 billion | Global bridge language |
| 2 | Mandarin Chinese | `zh` | ~1.1 billion | Largest single language group |
| 3 | Hindi | `hi` | ~600–700 million | Major South Asian language |
| 4 | Spanish | `es` | ~550–600 million | Americas + Spain |
| 5 | Arabic (MSA) | `ar` | ~400 million+ | Shared written form across many countries |
| 6 | French | `fr` | ~300 million | Africa + Europe + Canada |
| 7 | Portuguese | `pt` | ~260 million | Brazil + Portugal + Africa |
| 8 | Russian | `ru` | ~250 million | Original language of the cycle |
| 9 | Indonesian / Malay | `id` | ~200+ million | Southeast Asia |
| 10 | Bengali | `bn` | ~300 million | Bangladesh + India |

**Combined coverage of these 10 languages significantly exceeds 75%** of global adult literate population when second-language speakers are included.

## Translation principles

1. **Meaning over literalism** — especially for philosophical tone.
2. Keep the five-book structure identical across languages.
3. Preserve the core metaphors (mosaic, Stage, Engineer, subordinating entropy).
4. Allow natural rhythm in each language.
5. Credit translators.
