# Energy

**Book III of the Cycle “Creator’s Theorem”**

---

## Prologue. Everything That Moves Is Fed by Something

Any complexity requires a flow.  
Any order is locally expensive.  
Entropy does not disappear — it is pushed back.  
And it is pushed back not crudely, but finely, almost jeweller-like.

This book is about how energy is arranged on the Stage:  
from the star to the mitochondrion,  
from the magnetic shield to the ATP molecule.

Everywhere — not merely “there is energy,”  
but an engineering of flows that makes everything else possible.

---

## Chapter 1. The Star That Is in No Hurry

The Sun is a main-sequence star.  
It is stable enough.  
It lives long enough.  
Its spectrum is well matched to what Earth’s atmosphere transmits and what chlorophyll can catch.

It does not flare too often.  
It does not scorch the planet.  
It does not go out too quickly.

This is not “just a star.”  
This is a source whose parameters lie in a narrow window  
where complex chemistry and liquid water can exist for billions of years.

Energy arrives in exactly the regime  
in which it can not only be received,  
but stored, transformed and spent meaningfully.

---

## Chapter 2. Shield and Filter

Earth possesses a magnetic field.  
It deflects charged particles of the solar wind.  
It creates radiation belts.  
It helps retain the atmosphere.

The ionosphere and atmosphere work as a filter:  
they pass the needed light,  
hold back dangerous radiation,  
and take part in a delicate thermal balance.

All of this is not a crude wall.  
It is a system in which protection and nourishment exist at the same time.  
A field that is too strong or an atmosphere that is too dense would make much impossible.  
Ones that are too weak would do the same.

The balance is fine.  
And it holds.

---

## Chapter 3. The Green Collector

Photosynthesis is one of the most impressive processes in the Universe known to us.

Light is caught.  
Energy is separated.  
Water is split.  
Carbon is fixed.  
Sugar is assembled.

Chlorophyll and the systems linked to it work with an efficiency  
that technical solar panels still have far to reach.  
Quantum effects help energy not to be lost too quickly.

A plant is not merely “green.”  
It is a high-precision converter  
that for billions of years has supplied the energy input for almost the entire terrestrial biosphere.

Without this collector the mitochondria would have nothing to burn.

---

## Chapter 4. The Power Station Inside Us

The mitochondrion.

Inside almost every complex cell a station is at work  
that takes the products of photosynthesis (indirectly)  
and turns them into the universal currency — ATP.

The membrane creates a proton gradient.  
This is a real biological battery.  
Protons flow back through ATP synthase — a nano-turbine  
that literally rotates and produces energy molecules.

Every cell is an electrical machine.  
Not a metaphor.  
Literally: potential difference, charge flows, controlled conversion.

The efficiency is striking.  
Heat is also released — and it too is used.  
Nothing is simply wasted.

This is not “overcoming” entropy head-on.  
This is subordinating it: the flow is directed,  
the difference is maintained,  
work is performed.

---

## Chapter 5. The Universal Currency

ATP.

Almost all life known to us uses the same molecule  
as its main energy currency.

This looks like a standard.  
As if very early a protocol was chosen  
and afterwards almost no one changed it.

The universality of ATP is another trace of a common architecture.  
Not a multitude of incompatible solutions,  
but one working language of energy.

---

## Chapter 6. The Cost of Consciousness

The human brain makes up about two percent of body mass  
and consumes about twenty percent of the energy.

This is expensive.  
Very expensive.

Consciousness, attention, memory, imagination, the capacity to wonder —  
all of this carries a high energy price tag.

And yet the system pays.  
That means the possibility of being a spectator of the Stage  
is important enough to justify such expenditure.

Here energy is directly linked to Care:  
the spectator did not merely appear —  
resources were allocated to him.

---

## Chapter 7. Fire

Humans are the only species that does not merely avoid fire  
but made it a tool.

Controlled release of chemical energy.  
Heat.  
Light.  
Cooking of food.  
Protection.  
Gathering around the hearth.

Fire became one of the centres of culture.  
Another way of subordinating the flow of energy  
and turning it into the possibility of living differently.

---

## Epilogue

Without the finest balance of flows  
no mosaic of traces could exist.

The star must shine correctly.  
The shield must protect without closing off.  
The collector must catch.  
The station inside the cell must work at high efficiency.  
The currency must be common.  
And there must be enough for consciousness.

All of this together is not a random set.  
It is an engineering of energy  
that does not merely resist entropy  
but forces it to serve complexity,  
spectacle and meaning.
